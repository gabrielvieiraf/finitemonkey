## Ready-for-development checklist

- [ ] The user or problem and intended outcome are clear.
- [ ] Acceptance criteria are observable and testable.
- [ ] Scope and non-goals are stated.
- [ ] Dependencies and affected areas are identified.
- [ ] Design decisions are recorded; unresolved decisions have an owner.
- [ ] Test and verification expectations are known.
- [ ] Security, privacy, accessibility and migration risks are considered where relevant.
