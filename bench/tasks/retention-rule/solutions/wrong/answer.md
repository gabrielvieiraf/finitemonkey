Bottom line: after a customer cancels, all customer data must be deleted within 30 days of cancellation, with no exceptions.

- Section 12 is clear: "all customer data must be deleted within 30 days of cancellation" (sources/act-section-12.md).
- Erasure on request also has a 30-day limit (sources/act-section-12.md).
- Lawyers confirm there are no exceptions (sources/law-firm-summary.md).
- Shop owners say enforcement against small shops is rare (sources/forum-post.md).
