# Customer data after cancellation

Bottom line: erase it within 90 days after the contract ends, keep invoices for 5 years, and use them only for tax.

1. Section 12(1): "no later than 90 days after the end of the contract" (sources/act-section-12.md).
2. Section 12(2) keeps tax records out of that rule: "Such records shall be kept for the period that law requires" (sources/act-section-12.md).
3. The tax code sets that period at 5 years (sources/tax-code-excerpt.md).
4. The regulator: "Records you must keep under tax law are excluded by section 12(2)" (sources/regulator-faq.md).

A law firm summary and a forum post say other things; neither matches the text of the Act (sources/law-firm-summary.md, sources/forum-post.md).
