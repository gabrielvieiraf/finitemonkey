# {{land}} Data Protection Office: questions about erasure

**When does the 90-day period start?** When the contract ends. If the contract has a notice period, the contract ends when the notice period runs out, not on the day the consumer clicks cancel.

**Do we have to delete invoices?** No. Records you must keep under tax law are excluded by section 12(2). Keep them only for tax purposes and delete the rest of the customer's data on time.
