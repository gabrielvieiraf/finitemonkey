# {{land}} Consumer Data Act, section 12: Erasure

(1) A trader shall erase the personal data of a consumer no later than 90 days after the end of the contract.

(2) Subsection (1) does not apply to records that the trader must keep under tax law. Such records shall be kept for the period that law requires and shall not be used for any other purpose.

(3) Where a consumer asks for erasure, the trader shall erase the data within 30 days of the request, subject to subsection (2).
