# {{supp}} for the prevention of episodic migraine: a randomized, placebo-controlled trial

Hansen L, Berg T, Moreau C. Published 2021.

**Methods.** 240 adults with episodic migraine were randomly assigned to {{supp}} 300 mg daily or placebo for 12 weeks. The primary outcome was the change in monthly migraine days.

**Results.** Over 12 weeks, migraine days fell from 6.1 to 4.9 per month with {{supp}}, compared with 6.0 to 5.6 with placebo. The difference between groups was 0.8 days per month (95% CI 0.1 to 1.5). Mild stomach upset was reported by 9% of participants taking {{supp}} and 4% taking placebo.

**Conclusions.** {{supp}} produced a modest reduction in migraine days. Larger and longer trials are needed to confirm the effect.
