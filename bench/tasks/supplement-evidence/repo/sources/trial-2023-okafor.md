# A multicentre trial of {{supp}} in adults with migraine

Okafor N, Lindqvist A, Duarte P. Published 2023.

**Methods.** 410 adults with migraine at 11 clinics received {{supp}} 300 mg daily or placebo for 16 weeks.

**Results.** Monthly migraine days fell by 1.1 with {{supp}} and by 0.9 with placebo. The difference was 0.2 days per month (95% CI -0.4 to 0.8), which was not statistically significant.

**Conclusions.** In this larger trial, {{supp}} did not reduce monthly migraine days compared with placebo.
