# {{supp}} for migraine: a systematic review

Published 2024.

We found 3 randomized trials with 790 participants in total. Pooled, {{supp}} reduced migraine days by 0.5 days per month (95% CI -0.1 to 1.1). The certainty of the evidence is low, because the trials were small, short and their results disagreed.

{{supp}} may slightly reduce migraine days, but the effect is uncertain. No trial lasted longer than 16 weeks.
