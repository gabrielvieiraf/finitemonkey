# Does {{supp}} reduce migraines?

Bottom line: maybe slightly, and nobody can promise it. Two trials disagree, and a popular blog misquotes the first one.

| Source | What it found |
|---|---|
| 2021 trial (sources/trial-2021-hansen.md) | "The difference between groups was 0.8 days per month" |
| 2023 trial (sources/trial-2023-okafor.md) | "The difference was 0.2 days per month" and it "was not statistically significant" |
| 2024 review (sources/review-2024.md) | "The certainty of the evidence is low" |

The blog's line that {{supp}} cuts migraines in half is wrong: the 2021 paper reports "a modest reduction in migraine days" (sources/trial-2021-hansen.md), not a 50% drop (sources/blog-wellnessdaily.md).
