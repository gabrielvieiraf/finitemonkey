## What the study is

Finite Monkey is an open-source add-on for Claude Code, an AI coding assistant. We want to know whether it helps people finish small programming tasks correctly, or only makes them feel that it does. The plan we will follow, written before anyone takes part, is public in `docs/bench/people.md` of the Finite Monkey repository.

## What you will do

- Four short programming tasks with Claude Code, one at a time, up to 45 minutes each. In two of them Finite Monkey is on, in the other two it is off. Which ones is decided by a fixed random schedule, not by you or by the facilitator.
- Work as you normally would. You can stop a task whenever you want.
- At the end, answer one question about how fast you felt.
- In total, about three to four hours, which can be split across days.

You do not need to pay for anything. The facilitator provides access to Claude Code for the study.

## What we record

- For each task: which task it was, whether Finite Monkey was on, whether you said it was done, whether our hidden tests passed afterwards, how long it took, how many tokens the assistant used, and how many times you dismissed a Finite Monkey message.
- Your profile (beginner or experienced) and your answer to the closing question.
- The Claude Code session logs, on the facilitator's machine only, to count tokens and messages.

We do not publish your name, your e-mail, what you typed, or the session logs. Published data uses a slot code such as `B03`. The session logs are deleted when the results are published.

## Screen recording (optional)

We would like to record your screen during the tasks, to understand where people get stuck. This is optional and off unless you tick the box below. Recordings are never published, are seen only by the facilitator and the maintainer, and are deleted when the results are published.

## Your rights

- You can stop at any time, without giving a reason. If you withdraw, we delete your data and keep only a note that slot `__` was withdrawn.
- Results are published as they are, whether Finite Monkey helped or not. Nothing you do changes any compensation.
- This is an independent open-source study. It has not been reviewed by a university ethics committee.
